I offer

English Tutoring

I am trained and experienced in helping teenage and adult ESOL learners progress in secondary school, work and everyday life. I can also provide tutoring for native English speakers who wish to improve their grammar and standard of English, as well as a proofreading service, checking for grammar and syntax errors in documents.

Online or in-person

I offer online tutoring using Zoom. If you live in the Bromsgrove / Redditch / Droitwich area of the UK I can also provide face-to-face sessions for local households and workplaces.

All levels

I teach English to all levels of students from secondary school age to adults.

Personalised lessons

I offer bespoke online sessions (made just for you) for individuals, pairs or groups in which we work on areas of your choice. Improve your spoken English, your grammar or your writing… I can help you with whatever you need!
