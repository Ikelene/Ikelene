About Me

Qualifications

I hold a CELTA Level 5 Certificate for teaching English as a foreign language. I hold a current enhanced DBS certificate. I also have 2 undergraduate degrees:

  1. French, German and European Studies
  2. Social Psychology

Experience

I have teaching experience both in the classroom and online.

I currently work part-time as an ESOL teacher and a Functional Skills English teacher at a local college, as well as a private tutor.

Love of languages

I have enjoyed learning new languages since I started learning French at the age of 8. I spent 2 years in Austria in my teenage years and became fluent in German. I also have some knowledge of Italian, Spanish, Japanese, Ukrainian and Norwegian – this gives me a better understanding of how best to learn a language but also to identify the problems that learners of different nationalities may have with learning English.

Psychology background

My degree in psychology has helped me to understand the importance of cultural identity and learning a new language is very much part of this. I have moved to a foreign country myself and experienced learning a new language and integrating into a new culture. Certain aspects of psychology can also be exploited to make learning a new language easier.
